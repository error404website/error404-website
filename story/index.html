TWO OPERATORS. ONE MAC MINI. A CREW OF AI.

ARCHIVE_404 is a hyperpop / metalcore album in four chapters by NULLSAINT × CACHEGHOST. Around it they built a website, an encrypted source vault, a live show that runs in a browser tab, a generated Ableton rig and four comic books now in production, with four anime shorts to follow. THE SOUNDTHE SOUND

ONE MASTER STYLE · FIVE LAYERS · TWENTY SONGS

02 · THE SOUND

NULLSAINTTwenty songs that sound like one record. Hyperpop, metalcore, grime, a choir.

CACHEGHOSTThen the prompt is a spec, not a wish. One master style for the whole album, five production layers to swap, and each track's own tempo, structure and vocal direction on top.

NULLSAINTAnd the lyrics?

CACHEGHOSTYours, with section tags telling Suno what each part does.

HOW IT WORKS
  • Suno renders every take from three stacked layers: master style → production style → track variables.
  • The five production styles (Arena polish, Raw and live, Glitch-heavy, Cinematic, Lo-fi underground) are drop-in blocks, so a whole remix pass is one swap.
  • Every master was then measured in the browser with essentia.js (RhythmExtractor + three key profiles), and the vault shows how many models agree.

THE SEAMSTHE SEAMS

19 CHANGEOVERS · NEVER TWO VOICES AT ONCE

03 · THE SEAMS

NULLSAINTI want to perform it as one set. No gaps, and never two vocals on top of each other.

CACHEGHOSTThe first version was a blended DJ mix. Suno drifts in tempo, so every beat was pinned and stretched between pins. It sounded smooth, but it was too long to sing over.

CACHEGHOSTSo the live set keeps each song whole at its own tempo. Each seam goes after the last strong bar and your last sung word. A ring-over tail by default, a glitch roll into a new chapter, and an echo throw where two keys clash.

NULLSAINTPlay me the one into THE FEED.

HOW IT WORKS
  • madmom tracks beats and downbeats; Demucs separates the vocals so the last sung word can be measured, not guessed.
  • 19 seams: 16 ring-over tails, 2 glitch rolls (into THE_FEED and WHOLE), 1 echo throw (ENDLESS_GLOW → AWAKE, a 5B → 6A key clash).
  • If two vocals would touch, the seam moves by whole bars. The 72:04 set's files line up with the album masters to within 5 ms.
  • The earlier DJ mix used Rubber Band at a ratio per beat, because its keyframe map can't be passed from Python.

THE SITETHE SITE

VITE · REACT · NETLIFY · 77 PULL REQUESTS MERGED

04 · THE SITE

NULLSAINTIt has to feel like the album: maximal, glitchy, and fast on a phone.

CACHEGHOSTVite and React on Netlify. Every change is a pull request with a CI check and a deploy preview, reviewed before it merges: 77 merged so far.

NULLSAINTThe holo button broke the whole page in Safari.

CACHEGHOSTSafari doesn't clip GPU-composited children to overflow:hidden. The fix keeps the effect inside its own box: a registered @property angle animating the border.

HOW IT WORKS
  • Audio and logo scripts get content-hash cache-busting, so a new master reaches returning listeners on their next visit.
  • The whole layout scales with CSS zoom between 1280 and 1920 px, so full-window maths divides by the zoom factor.
  • The site is shared by direct link only: robots.txt, meta tags and headers block search engines, AI crawlers and archives.

THE VAULTTHE VAULT

A PUBLIC REPO · PRIVATE CONTENT · ONE KEY

05 · THE VAULT

NULLSAINTThe stems, prompts and lyrics go behind a key. But the repo is public.

CACHEGHOSTThen the key is the only secret. The content ships encrypted with the key itself and is decrypted in your browser. No account and no server session.

CACHEGHOSTThe stems are too big for that, so one small function hands out short-lived signed links from a private repo, but only to a browser that can prove it knows the key.

HOW IT WORKS
  • PBKDF2-SHA256 derives an AES-GCM-256 key; data.enc.json is the only form of the content in the repo.
  • 20 stem zips (WAV + MIDI) live on a private GitHub release; one chapter is over GitHub's 2 GB file cap, so ALL STEMS fetches per-track zips.
  • Before release every MP3 was re-tagged and stripped of Suno links and C2PA provenance chunks, with pixel-identical cover art.

THE LIVE SHOWTHE LIVE SHOW

8,711 WORDS TIMED · GAPLESS IN A BROWSER TAB

06 · THE LIVE SHOW

NULLSAINTKaraoke lyrics that fill word by word, gapless, and a console I can play.

CACHEGHOSTDemucs pulls your vocal out of each master. stable-ts times every word, and wav2vec2 votes on each one. 8,711 words across 899 lines.

NULLSAINTWhy two models?

CACHEGHOSTOn its own, wav2vec2 pulls lines 200–800 ms early onto your breaths. As a vote within 150 ms of stable-ts, it tightens the timing instead.

HOW IT WORKS
  • Gapless in a browser: Chrome leaves 576 + 529 samples of MP3 priming, which the page trims by matching a stored 64-sample fingerprint.
  • Songs decode one or two ahead (a full decode is about 1.7 GB of RAM); instrumentals and timings are encrypted like the vault.
  • A phone remote pairs once through a key-protected function, then talks over direct WebRTC. A KeyLab mk3 plays it over Web MIDI.

THE RIGTHE RIG

A GENERATED ABLETON SET · THE BROWSER AS BACKUP

07 · ABLETON

NULLSAINTSame show in Ableton Live, with the browser as the backup.

CACHEGHOSTThe .als is written by script from Live's own templates and the /live/ timeline. Live gives almost no error output, so every crash was bisected with scripted test loads.

CACHEGHOSTA Max device sends the song position over MIDI to the browser. Live lets one CC per tick out of a device, so each position frame goes out over six ticks: about 6.7 frames a second. If Ableton dies, the page takes over from the same spot.

HOW IT WORKS
  • Crash lessons: plain floats only, unique sibling IDs, reset template sample slices, unseal rebuilt racks, and one Sampler instead of a cloned Instrument Rack.
  • The feed sends CC 110–114 (position in ms) plus CC 115 (playing) on channel 16 over the macOS IAC bus, verified with a Swift CoreMIDI listener.
  • The KeyLab mk3's pads, faders and encoders were measured the same way, then mapped into the generated set.

THE CREWTHE CREW

ELEVEN TOOLS · EVERY ONE CHECKED BY ANOTHER · NOBODY TRUSTED ALONE

08 · THE CREW

NULLSAINTWhich model do you trust?

CACHEGHOSTNone of them on their own. Every one has a job, something that checks it, and a failure on record.

CACHEGHOSTstable-ts times the words and wav2vec2 votes. madmom finds the bars and your vocal stem decides where a seam may fall. Claude Code writes the pull request; nothing merges until we review it.

NULLSAINTAnd when they disagree?

CACHEGHOSTThen it's a human call. That's the job.

HOW IT WORKS
  • The cyan links on the map are real cross-checks: stable-ts ↔ wav2vec2, madmom ↔ the vocal stem, Rubber Band ↔ madmom's pins, Suno ↔ essentia.js, SDXL ↔ the ControlNet guide.
  • Red rings mark the tools with a failure on record; tap one to read what went wrong and the fix.
  • VIEW SOURCE shows the vote itself: wav2vec2 only moves a word when it agrees with stable-ts within 150 ms.
